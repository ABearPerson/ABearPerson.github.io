About me

I am a PhD candidate at the National University of Singapore (NUS), advised by Jialin Li. I also served as a full-time teaching assistant at NUS from 2021 to early 2026.

I previously earned a bachelor’s degree in Computer Science (Information Security) with First Class Honours from NUS. My final-year project was supervised by Min Suk Kang, with Dinil Mon Divakaran as my co-advisor.

My research to date has focused on Byzantine fault-tolerant algorithms and protocols. I remain interested in this area and in finding practical applications for theoretical cryptography. I am also keen to explore new topics and take on new challenges. If you are interested in working together, feel free to reach out! 🙂

I expect to graduate in early 2027 and am currently exploring job opportunities.
